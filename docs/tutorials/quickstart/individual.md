# Quickstart: Individual

## Goal
Create your first inheritance plan in under 15 minutes.

## Steps
1. Choose a blockchain (EVM or Solana).
2. Add assets and beneficiaries.
3. Select a legal framework.
4. Configure the Dead Man's Switch.
5. Generate and review the contract + legal PDF.

## Tips
- Start with testnet assets.
- Use a simple distribution for your first plan.
