# Quickstart: Individual

## Goal

Create your first inheritance plan on heir.es.

## Steps

1. Open https://heir.es/login
2. After `/welcome`, unpaid users land `/dashboard` and pay; or try `/interview/try` then sign up
3. Paid home is `/interview` ($88 Legacy Interview on `/pricing`)
4. When the film/conversation completes, continue at `/legacy`
5. Desk work is `/desk` (paid). Do not go to `/swarm`.

See [Product paths](/guide/product-paths). Not legal advice.

## Tips

- Start with the live SKUs on [heir.es/pricing](https://heir.es/pricing).
- `/interview/try` is local-only; it is not a free interview.
