# Glossary

- **Dead Man's Switch**: Timer-based inheritance trigger after inactivity.
- **Mirath**: Quranic fixed-share inheritance system.
- **Wasiyyah**: Islamic bequest up to one-third of the estate.
- **Waqf**: Charitable endowment structure.
- **Per Stirpes**: Distribution by family branch.
- **Per Capita**: Equal distribution among beneficiaries.
- **ERC-6551**: Token-bound account standard.
