# FAQ

## Common Questions

**What blockchains does HEIR support?**
EVM chains (Ethereum, Polygon, Base, Avalanche), Solana, and TON.

**How does the Dead Man's Switch work?**
It triggers inheritance after a configured inactivity period unless reset.

**Does HEIR support Islamic inheritance?**
Yes, Mirath, Wasiyyah, and Waqf templates are supported.

**Is HEIR non-custodial?**
Yes, HEIR does not store private keys.
